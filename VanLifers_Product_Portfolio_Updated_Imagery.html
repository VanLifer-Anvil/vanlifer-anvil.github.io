Steve English · Product development portfolio

Future product range opportunities.

This portfolio brings together a selection of products and product ranges I have been developing for the campervan, motorhome and wider leisure vehicle markets.

Many of these projects have already progressed through extensive design and engineering development, with engineering reports, materials and component specifications, supplier availability, sourcing links and prototype costings completed and organised in preparation for prototype manufacture. Others are approaching that stage.

Each has been developed with practical applications, commercial viability and potential market demand in mind. Together, they offer opportunities to broaden the VanLifers range beyond its existing connectivity products.
