Our Approach

Connectivity built around life on the road

Systems designed by vanlifers for vanlifers — not systems designed by people in offices.

A steel body can weaken the signal reaching equipment inside your van. A suitable roof-mounted aerial collects mobile signal and campsite Wi-Fi outside the vehicle.

Life on the road does not run to a schedule, and your connection should not either. We build every kit around how vanlifers actually travel, matching the router, the aerial and the SIM to your van and your budget. Nothing here is a one-size box pulled off a shelf, and if your needs change, the system grows with you.

You also get support from people who understand a steel-bodied van and a weak signal on a remote pitch. Every package is pre-configured and tested before it leaves us, so you are online from the first power-on. And if something is not right, you talk to a person, not a queue.

eSIM & shared data

Your van and your mobile phones: one shared data account?

PLACEHOLDER FOR ANVIL — Explanation and product terms to be supplied before publication. The questions below are editorial prompts, not confirmed service claims.

Get connected with an eSIM

Explain what an eSIM is, which phones support it, how it is delivered and activated, and the standalone plans available. Distinguish starter data from the full range of top-up allowances.

Link your mobile phones to your data account

Confirm whether the physical SIM in the router and additional phone eSIMs can share one allowance. Explain the number of linked SIMs, eligible plans, charges, top-ups and expiry rules in plain English.

Use your mobile phone away from the van

Explain whether each phone connects independently to available mobile networks, including while the router is switched off. Make clear the difference between sharing an account and connecting to the van’s Wi-Fi.

What happens to your existing number?

Confirm whether customers can replace their existing mobile SIM and port their number, with normal calls and texts, or whether this is data only and they must keep a separate mobile service for those functions.

Make calls using your shared data

Want to make calls when your usual network has no signal, or calls abroad aren’t included in your plan? Use a free app such as WhatsApp to call other users of the same app over your shared eSIM data. Data allowance and coverage apply.
